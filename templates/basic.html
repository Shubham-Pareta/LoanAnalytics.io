Key Features Analyzed

Number of Dependents

The number of individuals financially dependent on the applicant. More dependents might indicate a higher financial burden.

Annual Income

The total income earned by the applicant in a year. Higher income usually increases the likelihood of loan approval.

Loan Amount

The amount of money requested by the applicant. Larger loan amounts require careful assessment against the applicant’s financial stability.

Loan Term

The duration for which the loan is taken. Longer terms can mean higher interest rates and affect the approval decision.

CIBIL Score

A measure of the applicant’s creditworthiness. Higher scores reflect better credit history and increase the chances of approval.

Asset Values

The total value of the applicant’s assets, including residential, commercial, luxury, and bank assets. Higher asset values provide more security for the loan.

Education Level

Indicates whether the applicant is a graduate or not. Education level can influence income potential and financial stability.

Self Employment Status

Whether the applicant is self-employed. Self-employment status can impact income stability and loan approval.
